# Background-generation prompt

The atmosphere plate was generated with Codex's built-in ImageGen using
`docs/brand/branding-sheet.png` as a palette-and-personality reference. It is
not a complete Store asset: `build.py` adds every exact brand and product
element afterward.

> Use case: ads-marketing
> Asset type: background plate for the Meta Horizon Store universal cover and
> matching hero cover
> Primary request: Create ONLY an abstract, premium spatial background plate
> for the 00Widget Horizon OS panel app.
> Input images: the supplied 00Widget branding sheet is a
> palette-and-personality reference only; do not reproduce or reinterpret its
> logo, mascot, wordmark, tagline, typography, UI, or any recognizable object.
> Scene/backdrop: deep navy mixed-reality workspace atmosphere with graceful
> translucent signal ribbons and softly luminous spatial planes, a subtle
> sense of depth and horizon, calm central stage for exact brand art and
> dashboard-panel illustrations to be overlaid later.
> Style/medium: polished editorial 3D abstraction, friendly and sophisticated,
> precise enough for a major app storefront.
> Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape; keep the central 60% width and
> central 65% height dark, simple, and low-detail; place richer cyan/blue/teal
> energy around outer edges and corners; design must also tolerate a centered
> square and portrait crop.
> Lighting/mood: confident, calm, ambient; faint soft glow, no harsh neon.
> Color palette: deep navy #06152A, brand blue #1F8BFF and #0968E8, trend teal
> #1FB89A, restrained accent purple #5B35DB.
> Materials/textures: translucent glass-like ribbons, soft atmospheric depth,
> subtle organic grain.
> Constraints: edge-to-edge artwork; no typography, letters, numbers, glyphs,
> logos, mascot, people, headset, hardware, UI panels, interface, phone,
> borders, frames, black bars, watermark, stars, circuit boards, cyberpunk
> city, or generic stock-tech icons. The center must remain highly readable
> for later overlays.
