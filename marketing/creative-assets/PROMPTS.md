# Background-generation prompts

The two atmosphere plates were generated with Codex's built-in ImageGen using
`docs/brand/branding-sheet.png` as a palette-and-personality reference. They
are deliberately not complete creative assets: `build.py` adds every exact
brand and product element afterward.

## Product page header

> Create ONLY an abstract background plate for a premium App Store PRODUCT
> PAGE HEADER creative asset for the 00Widget app. Landscape canvas, exact
> target aspect ratio 3840:1646 (about 2.333:1). The supplied branding sheet is
> a palette-and-personality reference only: DO NOT reproduce, reinterpret,
> trace, or include the logo, fedora agent, wordmark, tagline, letters,
> numbers, icons, cards, phone, device, interface, or any recognizable object.
>
> Visual direction: deep navy #06152A base; restrained luminous currents in
> brand blue #1F8BFF / #0968E8, trend teal #1FB89A, and a smaller accent of
> purple #5B35DB. Sophisticated, friendly, slightly organic, modern
> Apple-platform polish. Think ambient signal paths and soft out-of-focus pools
> of light, with delicate paper-like grain and subtle depth—not generic neon
> cyberpunk, not a star field, not circuit-board lines. The center must remain
> calm, dark, and high-contrast because exact logo and authentic product UI
> will be overlaid later. Allow richer color and motion toward the far left and
> far right edges, but keep the central 43% width and central 40% height
> visually quiet. No typography, no glyphs, no mock UI, no frames, no borders,
> no watermark. Edge-to-edge artwork, no black bars.

## Search results

> Create ONLY an abstract background plate for a premium App Store SEARCH
> RESULTS creative asset for the 00Widget app. Landscape canvas, exact target
> aspect ratio 3840:2560 (3:2). The supplied branding sheet is a
> palette-and-personality reference only: DO NOT reproduce, reinterpret, trace,
> or include the logo, fedora agent, wordmark, tagline, letters, numbers, icons,
> cards, phone, device, interface, or any recognizable object.
>
> Visual direction: deep navy #06152A base with a crisp but friendly editorial
> sense of depth. Use brand blue #1F8BFF / #0968E8, trend teal #1FB89A, and a
> restrained accent purple #5B35DB as soft luminous ribbons or translucent
> atmospheric planes. This placement is viewed small, so simplify aggressively:
> one strong, calm central focal zone, high contrast, no visual noise. Keep the
> center safe region (roughly the middle 56% width and 40% height) dark and
> readable; concentrate the abstract color energy near the outer corners and as
> one subtle diagonal current that leads the eye left-to-right. Slight organic
> grain, refined Apple-platform polish, not cyberpunk, not a galaxy, not
> circuit-board art. No typography, no glyphs, no mock UI, no frames, no
> borders, no watermark. Edge-to-edge artwork, no black bars.
